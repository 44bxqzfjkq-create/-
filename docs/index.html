Daily Video Poster

Daily Video Poster is a personal tool that publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account on a schedule.

What it does

  • The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the tool to publish videos.
  • The tool uploads the owner's prepared video files with a caption and hashtags through the TikTok Content Posting API (Direct Post).
  • Up to three videos are posted per day.

The tool is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.
