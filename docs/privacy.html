Privacy Policy

Daily Video Poster ("the tool") is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy explains what data it handles.

Data the tool accesses

  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok after the account holder authorizes the tool (scope: video.publish).
  • Basic creator information returned by the Content Posting API (such as the account nickname and available privacy settings), used only at the moment of posting.
  • The video files, captions and hashtags that the owner chooses to publish.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to publish the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored in a private, access-restricted configuration and are deleted when the authorization is revoked. The tool does not collect or store data about other TikTok users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings > Security & permissions > Apps and services permissions.
